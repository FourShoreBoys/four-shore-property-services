Four Shore Property Services is a growing small business started by four friends from the Cumberland Beach area: Everett, Dylan, Dawson, and Justin.

We're working to build a business by helping neighbours with yard care, seasonal cleanups, snow shovelling, and the smaller jobs that need an extra pair of hands.

We try to communicate clearly, respect people's property, and only accept work we're equipped to do safely.
